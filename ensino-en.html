Teaching materials

I am gradually making my teaching materials (in Portuguese) available on my GitHub page. As of November 12, 2026, the courses with complete materials are “Machine Learning I”, “Statistics and Probability for Computer Science”, “Exploratory Data Analysis” and “Probability II”. I will keep adding the other courses I teach regularly, since organizing everything I have already prepared takes time. To request an editable version of the materials, contact me at the email address on the home page.

I am also developing, with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic’s AI model), interactive apps to support learning. The first one is Classificadores Interativos, with visualizations of the classifiers covered in Machine Learning I. This one and the ones to come are collected on the Apps page.
