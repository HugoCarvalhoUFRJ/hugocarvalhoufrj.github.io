Apps

Teaching tools & games

Small web apps I built with the assistance of Claude, Anthropic’s AI model: some to help students learn, others just for fun. All of them run in the browser.

Teaching

Classificadores Interativos Portuguese

Interactive visualizations of the classifiers covered in Aprendizagem de Máquina I (Machine Learning I) at UFRJ: Naive Bayes, LDA, QDA, maximal margin and support vector classifiers, SVM, decision trees, random forests and KNN. The apps grew out of students’ questions in class, and the notation follows An Introduction to Statistical Learning.

Open app

Games

Jogo da Encomenda Portuguese

“Todos sabem onde estão… menos um.”

A social deduction party game for 3 or more players, each on their own phone. Everyone shares a secret location except the impostor, and the questions go around: expose the impostor without giving the place away. Inspired by Spyfall.

Play

Agente Duplo Portuguese

“Toda palavra tem dois lados.”

A team word game: spymasters give clues to lead their team to its agents among 25 words, while steering clear of the double agent. One shared screen is the board, and each spymaster sees the secret key on their own phone. Inspired by Codenames.
